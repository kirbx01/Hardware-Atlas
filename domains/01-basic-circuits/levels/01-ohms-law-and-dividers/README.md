# Basic Circuits Level 01 -- Ohm's Law and Dividers

The first two builds in the domain: a resistor limiting current for an LED, and two resistors in series splitting a voltage. Together they make Ohm's law and Kirchhoff's current law measurable instead of memorised.

## Lessons

| 02 | [Voltage divider](../../../../lessons/02-voltage-divider/README.md) | Node voltage, and what a load does to it | ✅ |

## Why these two first

The divider single-handedly shows the load problem: a meter (or a resistor, or an ADC pin) changes the voltage it measures. That one lesson is why later domains check the source before trusting the number.
