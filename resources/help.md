# Where to Get Help

Show the circuit, schematic, code, supply voltage, expected result, measured result, and what you already tried. A photograph of a breadboard alone is rarely enough.

- **Beginner project ideas:** [Hack Club](https://hackclub.com/) for student-led making, [Hackster](https://www.hackster.io/) for project write-ups and board examples, [Instructables Circuits](https://www.instructables.com/circuits/projects) for build inspiration, and [Circuit Digest](https://circuitdigest.com/) for Indian electronics articles and projects.
- **Arduino and board-specific issues:** [Arduino Forum](https://forum.arduino.cc/) for Arduino hardware and libraries, [Espressif community](https://community.espressif.com/) and [Espressif documentation](https://docs.espressif.com/) for ESP32 issues, and [Raspberry Pi forums](https://forums.raspberrypi.com/) for Pico and Raspberry Pi support.
- **Advanced electronics:** [Electrical Engineering Stack Exchange](https://electronics.stackexchange.com/) for focused, reproducible engineering questions. Use relevant manufacturer forums and technical communities for datasheet-specific questions.

Search first, then ask one narrow question with a reproducible setup. Do not post private keys, serial numbers, or personal information.
