# Level 12: Semiconductor Fabrication

Fabrication is studied through process models, safe teaching facilities, and open documentation. It is not a home chemistry project.

## Project sequence

1. **Process-flow map:** document wafer preparation, oxidation, deposition, lithography, etch, implant, metallisation, and packaging.
2. **Lithography simulation:** model resolution, alignment, and process variation.
3. **Device cross-section:** connect process steps to a diode or MOSFET cross-section.
4. **Yield experiment:** simulate defects, process corners, and the effect on a small array.
5. **Packaging and test study:** relate thermal, electrical, and mechanical constraints to final test.

## Verify

Use facility safety rules, process documentation, microscopy or simulation outputs, yield calculations, and clear uncertainty bounds.
