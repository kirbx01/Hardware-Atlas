# Level 11: Semiconductor Devices

This level explains how device physics becomes the models used by circuit designers.

## Project sequence

1. **Diode characterisation:** measure forward voltage and current safely across a limited range.
2. **BJT curves:** compare cutoff, active, and saturation regions with a controlled test fixture.
3. **MOSFET transfer curves:** observe threshold variation and on-resistance at different gate voltages.
4. **Small-signal model:** compare a measured amplifier with a model and identify the limits of the approximation.
5. **SPICE model study:** vary temperature and process parameters to observe corners.

## Verify

Use current limits, documented fixtures, datasheets, SPICE plots, and uncertainty notes. Do not exceed device ratings for a teaching measurement.
